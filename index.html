Why the '60s Edition

VW revealed the Beetle Convertible at the 2012 Los Angeles Auto Show with three throwback editions, one each for the '50s, '60s, and '70s. The '60s Edition paired the turbo engine with Denim Blue paint and a matching two-tone leather interior.

It's also the last Beetle. VW ended production in 2019, so there is no newer generation to buy.

Expect lots of thumbs-ups at stoplights.

What it has

  • Power soft top

  • Two gloveboxes, one a nod to the original Beetle
  • Keyless entry and push-button start
  • Bluetooth and AUX input
  • Leather steering wheel and shift knob
  • Chrome trim inside and out
  • Sport-tuned suspension
  • ...and yes the A/C blows cold
